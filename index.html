HOW TO PLAYlearn the deep

You're a young electric eel in the twilight zone. Steer with your mouse or finger. Eat little fish to grow. Bite chunks off rival eels — or swallow smaller ones whole. Grab a starfish for a shield, and gulp a glowing baby electric eel to charge your zap. Anything that dies leaves energy behind — hoover it up to grow. Don't cross your own tail, or you'll bite yourself and shrink. Fish get you to level 2. After that a boss guards every level and killing it is the only way on — shock it, then ram it, once for every level you're on. A level 5 boss takes five hits. You get a breather after each one, so go and find a starfish.
